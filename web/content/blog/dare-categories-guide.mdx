DARE works best when both players are confident in their skill. Choosing the right category — and the right stake — is what makes a DARE fair, competitive, and worth winning.

Here's a breakdown of every category available at launch.

---

## Sports

The biggest category on DARE. Football juggling duels, sprint races, basketball one-on-one, and more.

**How proof works:** Upload a video clip of your performance. Timestamps and geo-tags are attached automatically. For head-to-head challenges, both players record at the same agreed location.

**Recommended stake range:** NGN 5,000 – 50,000

**Tips:** Agree on the exact format before accepting. "Most juggles in 60 seconds" is much cleaner than "best football skill" — specific terms mean faster verdicts.

---

## Gaming

FIFA seasons, chess matches, Ludo battles, mobile game high scores. If it runs on a device and separates skill from luck, it belongs here.

**How proof works:** Screen recordings are the standard. For turn-based games, the final board state or result screen is sufficient.

**Recommended stake range:** NGN 2,000 – 30,000

**Tips:** Agree on the platform and version. A FIFA 25 DARE played on different console generations is unfair. Clarify it in your terms before locking escrow.

---

## Fitness

Push-up counts, plank holds, burpee sets, pull-up maximums. Clean, objective, and hard to dispute.

**How proof works:** Front-facing video with full range of motion visible. DARE's jury checks rep form — only clean reps count.

**Recommended stake range:** NGN 1,000 – 20,000

**Tips:** Fitness DAREs settle fastest because form is easy to assess. Use them as starter DAREs to build your trust score before moving to higher-stakes categories.

---

## Creative

Freestyle rap, dance battles, beat-making, spoken word. The most subjective category — which is why jury quality matters most here.

**How proof works:** Video performance. For rap and spoken word, lyrics are included as a text submission alongside the clip.

**Recommended stake range:** NGN 5,000 – 100,000

**Tips:** Creative DAREs take longer to settle because jury deliberation is more involved. Factor that into your timeline. Higher stakes attract more experienced jurors.

---

## Mind

Trivia speed rounds, riddle-solving, debate challenges. Proof is in the answers.

**How proof works:** Screen recording of the trivia session or a structured Q&A format agreed in advance. Both players answer the same question set.

**Recommended stake range:** NGN 1,000 – 25,000

**Tips:** Specify the topic in your DARE terms. "General knowledge trivia" is harder to verify than "Premier League trivia 2010–2024". Narrow scope = faster settlement.

---

## Street

Hyper-local, geo-verified challenges. This is the most DARE-native category — designed for challenges that only make sense in a specific place at a specific time.

**How proof works:** Geo-location is mandatory. Both players must be within a defined radius when submitting proof. Location data is hashed and cannot be spoofed.

**Recommended stake range:** NGN 2,000 – 40,000

**Tips:** Street DAREs require both players to be physically present. They're perfect for neighbourhood rivalries, community events, and local tournaments.

---

## Choosing your stake

Start conservative. Build your trust score with a few smaller DAREs before moving to high-stakes challenges. A high trust score unlocks bigger stake limits, better matchmaking, and jury priority.

The best DAREs aren't the biggest ones — they're the ones where both players genuinely believe they'll win.
