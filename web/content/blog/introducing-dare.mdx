We built DARE because of a simple frustration: you'd watch a friend miss a free kick, and you'd both know — *you* could have scored that. There should be a way to back that claim with something real.

DARE is the answer.

## What is DARE?

DARE is a peer-to-peer skill wagering platform built for Africa. It lets you challenge anyone to a real-money competition in sports, gaming, fitness, or creative categories. You set the stakes, both players lock funds in escrow, and the winner is verified through evidence review or our blind jury system.

No house. No luck. Just skill.

## Why Africa?

Africa has the fastest-growing mobile gaming community in the world. Nigeria alone has over 30 million active mobile gamers. Yet there is no platform that lets them put their skill on the line with real, fair stakes.

We're starting with Nigeria and Kenya — two markets where mobile money, competitive culture, and digital-native youth overlap perfectly.

## How it works

1. **Create a DARE** — pick your category, set your wager, invite your opponent.
2. **Escrow locks** — both players confirm, and funds are held securely until settlement.
3. **Submit proof** — after completing the challenge, each player uploads their evidence.
4. **Court settles it** — a blind jury reviews the evidence and declares a winner. Funds release instantly.

## What makes it different

Most wagering platforms rely on chance — a spin, a match outcome you don't control. DARE is different. Every outcome is tied to a *skill you demonstrate*, not luck you hope for.

Our Predominance Test ensures every DARE category is genuinely skill-based. Our jury system ensures disputes are decided by evidence, not identity.
