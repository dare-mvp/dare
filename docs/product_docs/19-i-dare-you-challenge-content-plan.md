# I Dare You Challenge — Content Execution Plan

## Purpose

This document is the execution-ready content plan for the final phase of the I Dare You Challenge campaign. It covers all posts from now until slots close, ordered for maximum conversion impact.

It is a companion to [[18-make-it-a-dare-content-strategy]]. The "Make It A Dare" format is applied here specifically to drive challenge completions, not generic life goals.

---

## Campaign Context

The I Dare You Challenge has three reward tiers:

| Tier | Reward | Tasks required |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Standard | NGN 2,000 | Join waitlist + follow Instagram + share referral link + refer 2 friends |
| Champion | NGN 3,000 | Standard tasks + refer 3 friends + film a dare video with #IDareYouNG |
| Legend | NGN 9,000 | Champion tasks + refer 5 friends + film a dare battle + post in WhatsApp group of 20+ + join DARE WhatsApp channel |

**Status:** Less than 30 slots remaining. Every post from this point is a conversion post, not an awareness post.

---

## Strategic Shift

At this stage the audience already knows the challenge exists. The content job is no longer to introduce it. It is to remove the specific friction stopping mid-funnel people from completing each task.

Each post targets one of three blockers:

1. **Scarcity blindness** — people do not feel urgency because they assume slots will still be there tomorrow
2. **Task paralysis** — people know the challenge exists but have not started a specific task
3. **Social friction** — people need a social trigger (a dare from a friend, a video prompt, a WhatsApp nudge) to act

## Post 10 — Last Call UGC Video

**Type:** Higgsfield 2-person video
**Platforms:** Instagram Reels, TikTok, Twitter/X video
**Goal:** Emotional close. Show a real person who waited too long watching someone else claim the reward. Drive final slot fills.
**Trigger:** Generate and schedule when slots drop below 10. Publish same day as Post 9 or the day after.

### Scene brief

Woman asks man if he has done the DARE challenge yet. He says he has been meaning to but kept putting it off. She shows him her phone — Legend tier confirmed, ₦9,000 reward on screen. He immediately asks for the link. She sends it via WhatsApp. End card: "Slots almost gone. Link in bio."

### Higgsfield generation parameters

Follow all rules from the confirmed UGC production formula:

- **Model:** `marketing_studio_video`
- **Mode:** `ugc`
- **Hook:** Interview (`26cac2dd-99cb-4818-a678-509b0dab2c32`)
- **Product:** DareApp (`70b461e0-0746-4475-8abd-12d186755970`)
- **Souls:** Female `d92c9bd9` + Male `9b338936`, model `soul_2`
- **Aspect:** `9:16`
- **Duration:** 15s
- **Audio:** `generate_audio: true`

### Prompt rules

- Setting: flat natural daylight, worn concrete sidewalk, okada bikes passing, keke napep visible, low-rise buildings with sun-bleached walls, nothing polished or staged
- Dialogue: natural Nigerian English cadence — no Pidgin
- Phone direction: screen facing toward the man only — do not say "facing camera"
- No cinematic language: no golden hour, no amber light, no warm color grade, no HDR
- Both characters in the same frame throughout
- Show the app reward screen clearly when she holds up her phone

## Copy Rules (All Posts)

These apply to every post in this plan:

- Never say "only X days left" unless you know the exact closing date — use slot count instead
- Never imply guaranteed income or frame stakes as investment returns
- Always include the referral link or "link in bio" CTA
- Hashtag on every post: #IDareYouNG
- Tag: @dareappofficial where platform allows
- Keep copy under 150 words per post — this audience taps before reading

---

## What Not To Post During This Phase

- Generic brand awareness content — the window is too short for that
- Posts that explain what DARE is from scratch — mid-funnel content only
- Any post without a direct action the reader can take in under 2 minutes
- Reckless or unsafe dare suggestions in the battle video prompt
