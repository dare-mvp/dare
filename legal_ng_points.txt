The strongest option is a commercial technology law firm in Nigeria with fintech and gaming regulatory experience.

When contacting them, describe DARE as:

A mobile-first peer-to-peer skill challenge platform with optional real-money stakes, escrowed funds, answer-key verified gameplay, dispute resolution, KYC, wallet controls, and responsible play features.

Avoid describing it casually as “a betting app” or “Uber for betting.” That may push the conversation in the wrong direction before they understand the mechanics.

Ask them directly:

Have you advised Nigerian gaming, betting, lottery, fintech, payment, wallet, or prize-competition platforms before?

And:

Can you provide a written legal classification memo and licensing roadmap for launching this product in Nigeria, starting with Lagos?
